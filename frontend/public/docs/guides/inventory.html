Inventory Management Overview

Inventory Management lets your practice track medical supplies, medications, equipment, and consumables in real time. Monitor stock levels, record movements, manage purchase orders, and get alerted when items fall below reorder thresholds.

Getting to Inventory Management

  1. Log in as an Admin or authorised staff user
  2. Click Inventory in the top navigation
  3. The dashboard shows total items, low-stock count, total inventory value, and pending purchase orders

Dashboard at a Glance

CardWhat it shows
Total ItemsNumber of active inventory items
Low StockItems at or below reorder level
Total ValueEstimated inventory value at cost
Pending POsPurchase orders awaiting receipt

🏷️ Inventory Items

Items represent individual products your practice stocks — from gloves and syringes to medications and equipment.

Adding a New Item

  1. Go to Inventory → Items tab
  2. Click + New Item
  3. Fill in the required fields: Name, Item Type, and Unit of Measure
  4. Set Unit Cost and optional Selling Price
  5. Enter Reorder Level and Reorder Quantity to enable low-stock alerts
  6. Optionally assign a Category and Primary Supplier
  7. Enable tracking options (Lot/Batch, Expiry Date, Refrigeration) if needed
  8. Click Save

Item Types

TypeExamples
SupplyGloves, bandages, syringes
MedicationVaccines, injectables, oral medications
EquipmentBlood pressure cuffs, pulse oximeters
ConsumableReagents, test strips, single-use items

🔄 Stock Movements

Every change in stock quantity is recorded as a movement — this creates a full audit trail of your inventory.

Movement Types

TypeWhen to use
Receipt (In)Goods received from a supplier or PO
Issue (Out)Items used in patient care or procedures
AdjustmentCorrecting a count discrepancy
TransferMoving stock between locations or departments
ReturnReturning unused items to supplier

Recording a Movement

  1. Go to Inventory → Stock Movements tab
  2. Click + New Movement
  3. Select the item and movement type
  4. Enter quantity, date, and an optional lot/batch number
  5. Add a reason or reference note
  6. Click Save

Note: Receipts increase quantity on hand; issues and returns decrease it. Adjustments can go either direction.

🛒 Purchase Orders

Purchase Orders (POs) formalise requests to suppliers and let you track what has been ordered and received.

Creating a Purchase Order

  1. Go to Inventory → Purchase Orders tab
  2. Click + New PO
  3. Select the Supplier
  4. Set the expected delivery date
  5. Add line items: Item, Quantity, and Unit Cost
  6. Add any notes or terms
  7. Click Save as Draft or Submit Order

PO Statuses

StatusMeaning
DraftBeing prepared, not yet sent to supplier
PendingSubmitted and awaiting supplier confirmation
ApprovedConfirmed by supplier, goods in transit
OrderedOrder placed, awaiting delivery
PartialSome items received, remainder pending
ReceivedAll items delivered — auto-creates stock movements
CancelledOrder cancelled before fulfilment

Receiving a PO

When goods arrive, open the PO and click Mark as Received. The system automatically creates stock receipt movements for each line item, updating quantities on hand.

🚚 Suppliers

The Suppliers directory stores contact information for all vendors your practice orders from.

Adding a Supplier

  1. Go to Inventory → Suppliers tab
  2. Click + New Supplier
  3. Enter Name, Contact Person, Email, Phone, and Address
  4. Optionally add payment terms and notes
  5. Click Save

Suppliers appear in the Purchase Order form and can be linked as the primary supplier for individual items.

📂 Categories

Categories let you organise items into logical groups for filtering and reporting.

Adding a Category

  1. Go to Inventory → Categories tab
  2. Click + New Category
  3. Enter a category code (e.g. MED) and name (e.g. Medications)
  4. Add a description and set the active status
  5. Click Save

⚠️ Low Stock Alerts

AureonCare automatically flags items whose quantity on hand is at or below the configured reorder level.

Viewing Low-Stock Items

  1. Go to Inventory → Dashboard
  2. The Low Stock card shows the count — click it to filter the item list
  3. Or go to Items tab and filter by status Low Stock

Setting Reorder Levels

Edit any item and set Reorder Level (minimum quantity before reordering) and Reorder Quantity (how much to order). When stock drops to or below the reorder level, the item status automatically updates to Low Stock.

Reordering Quickly

  1. From the Items list, select low-stock items
  2. Click Create Purchase Order to auto-populate a PO with the item and reorder quantity
  3. Select the supplier and submit
