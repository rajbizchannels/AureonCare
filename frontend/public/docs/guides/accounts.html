Accounts Management Overview

Accounts Management gives your practice a complete double-entry general ledger. Track every financial transaction, manage AR and AP, reconcile bank accounts, and generate standard financial statements — all from within AureonCare.

Getting to Accounts Management

  1. Log in as an Admin or Finance role user
  2. Click Accounts in the top navigation
  3. The dashboard shows AR, AP, Cash Balance, and draft journal entries at a glance

Dashboard at a Glance

CardWhat it shows
Total ARSum of all open receivable balances
Total APSum of all pending payable amounts
Cash BalanceCurrent balance of cash/bank accounts
Draft EntriesJournal entries awaiting posting

📋 Chart of Accounts

The Chart of Accounts (COA) is the master list of accounts used by your practice's general ledger. Each account belongs to one of five types: Asset, Liability, Equity, Revenue, or Expense.

Adding a New Account

  1. Go to Accounts → Chart of Accounts tab
  2. Click + New Account
  3. Enter the account code (e.g. 1100)
  4. Enter the account name (e.g. Cash – Checking)
  5. Select the account type
  6. Set whether the account is currently active
  7. Click Save

Standard Account Numbering

RangeTypeExamples
1000–1999AssetsCash, AR, Equipment
2000–2999LiabilitiesAP, Loans payable
3000–3999EquityOwner's equity, Retained earnings
4000–4999RevenuePatient services revenue
5000–5999ExpensesSalaries, Rent, Supplies

📝 Journal Entries

Journal entries are the core of double-entry bookkeeping. Every entry must have equal debits and credits before it can be posted.

Creating a Journal Entry

  1. Go to Accounts → Journal Entries tab
  2. Click + New Entry
  3. Enter the entry date and a description/memo
  4. Add line items — each line needs an Account, a Debit or Credit amount, and a description
  5. Verify the entry is balanced (Debit total = Credit total)
  6. Click Save as Draft to save without posting, or Post Entry to commit it to the ledger

Entry Statuses

StatusMeaning
DraftSaved but not yet posted — can be edited or deleted
PostedCommitted to the ledger — creates an immutable audit trail
VoidedReversed by a system-generated reversing entry

Voiding an Entry

Posted entries cannot be deleted. To reverse one, use the Void action. The system creates an equal and opposite reversing entry automatically.

📈 Accounts Receivable (AR)

AR tracks money that patients and insurers owe your practice for services already rendered.

Viewing AR Records

  1. Go to Accounts → Accounts Receivable tab
  2. The list shows all open AR items with patient name, original amount, amount paid, and balance due
  3. Filter by status: Open, Partial, Paid, Written Off

AR Statuses

StatusMeaning
OpenFull balance outstanding
PartialSome payment received, balance remains
PaidFully collected
Written OffBalance deemed uncollectible
DisputedPatient or insurer is contesting the charge

Creating an AR Record

Click + New AR, select the patient, enter the original amount, due date, and reference (e.g. claim or invoice number). AR records link automatically to claims posted from Revenue Cycle Management.

📉 Accounts Payable (AP)

AP tracks bills your practice owes to vendors, suppliers, and service providers.

Adding a Bill

  1. Go to Accounts → Accounts Payable tab
  2. Click + New AP
  3. Select the vendor/payee
  4. Enter invoice number, amount, and due date
  5. Choose the expense account to debit
  6. Click Save

Recording a Payment

Open an AP record and click Record Payment. Enter the payment date and amount. The system updates the balance due and changes the status to Paid when fully settled.

✅ Bank Reconciliation

Bank reconciliation matches your practice's ledger transactions against your actual bank statement to catch errors and ensure accuracy.

Running a Reconciliation

  1. Go to Accounts → Reconciliation tab
  2. Click + New Reconciliation
  3. Select the bank/cash account and enter the statement date
  4. Enter the closing balance from your bank statement
  5. Check off each transaction that appears on the statement
  6. The system calculates the difference — aim for $0.00
  7. Once balanced, click Complete Reconciliation

Reconciliation Statuses

StatusMeaning
In ProgressReconciliation started, not yet completed
CompletedBalanced and locked
DiscrepancyCompleted with a non-zero difference flagged for review

📊 Financial Statements

Generate standard financial reports directly from your general ledger data.

Available Statements

ReportPurpose
Income Statement (P&L)Shows revenue, expenses, and net income for a period
Balance SheetSnapshot of assets, liabilities, and equity at a date
Trial BalanceLists all accounts with debit/credit totals to verify balance

Generating a Statement

  1. Go to Accounts → Statements tab
  2. Click + New Statement
  3. Select report type and date range
  4. Click Generate
  5. View on-screen or export to PDF/Excel via the Reports module
