The case for one system

Every handoff between systems is a place where money and safety leak.

A typical practice books in one tool, charts in another, bills through a clearinghouse portal and keeps its accounts somewhere else again. The patient's details are retyped at every boundary. Each retype costs staff time, and each slip shows up later as a denied claim, a missed allergy or a payment nobody can match.

AureonCare removes the boundaries. One record follows the patient from the first booking to the last payment, and every department works on that same record. The front desk, the clinician, the billing team and the finance office are looking at one set of facts.

Intake before arrival

Intake packets and consent forms are completed ahead of the visit and land on the chart. Every form version is kept.

Outreach

Medical camps

Plan the venue, services, staff, stations and supplies. Register walk-ins at the gate with a queue token, watch every station's queue live, and close with an outcomes report showing how many attendees went on to book at the practice.

Rostering a clinician who already has clinic appointments that day is flagged before it happens. The sponsor report holds counts only, with small groups combined so no one can be identified.

Configuration

Specialty setup

Starter content for 36 specialties: services, appointment types and forms. A preview shows exactly what will change before anything is written.

Anything the practice has already edited is reported and left alone. A later update will not restore the vendor's version over yours.

Operations

Locations

Clinics, community venues and mobile units are all sites. One is primary, and deactivating a site keeps every record that already refers to it.

Mobile

The mobile app

One app for phone and tablet, with four shells chosen by role: patient, clinician, front desk and administrator. It enforces the same permissions as the web.

Governance and security

Know who opened a chart, and show why they were allowed to.

Governance is part of the product. The answers a board, an auditor or a patient will ask for are kept as the work happens, rather than assembled afterwards.

An audit trail that records reads

Every create, update, delete and export is logged with the user, the time and the address. So is every view: opening a chart leaves a trace.

Access by role

View, create, edit and delete rights set per role and per module, with custom roles for the way the practice is organised.

Two-step sign-in

Authenticator-app verification at sign-in, enrolled from a QR code.

Sealed backups

Backups are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before they leave the server and can be stored in Google Drive or OneDrive. A restore touches only the practice's own data and must be confirmed.

Archiving by rule

Older records move to an archive under rules the practice sets, and can be restored when they are needed again.

No lock-in

Records export as FHIR R4. A diagnosis becomes a Condition and a vital sign an Observation, readable by any system that speaks the standard.
