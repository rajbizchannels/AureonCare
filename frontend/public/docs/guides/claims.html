Insurance Claims Management

Claims management is the process of submitting and tracking insurance claims for reimbursement of healthcare services.

Creating Claims

  1. Go to Revenue Cycle Management module
  2. Click Claims tab
  3. Click + New Claim
  4. Fill in claim details

Auto-Generated Claims

Claims automatically generate from:

  • Completed appointments
  • Signed encounter notes
  • Procedures performed

Eligibility Verification

  1. Click Verify Coverage button
  2. System contacts payer real-time
  3. Returns: Coverage status, deductible, copay, coinsurance

Submitting Claims

Electronic (EDI 837)

  1. Review claim for accuracy
  2. Click Submit Electronically
  3. Sent via clearinghouse
  4. Receive acknowledgment (997/999)

Paper (CMS-1500)

  1. Click Print CMS-1500
  2. Review for accuracy
  3. Mail to payer

Claim Status

  • Draft: Created, not submitted
  • Ready: Validated, awaiting submission
  • Submitted: Sent to payer
  • Acknowledged: Payer received
  • In Process: Payer processing
  • Accepted: Approved for payment
  • Paid: Payment received
  • Denied: Rejected by payer

Handling Denials

Common denial reasons:

  • Coverage terminated
  • Services not covered
  • Missing/invalid diagnosis
  • Medical necessity not met
  • Timely filing exceeded

Steps to appeal:

  1. Click denied claim
  2. Review denial code
  3. Click Appeal Denial
  4. Upload supporting documents
  5. Write appeal letter
  6. Submit appeal

Payment Posting

When payment received:

  1. System receives ERA (Electronic Remittance)
  2. Post payment amounts
  3. Apply adjustments/write-offs
  4. Update patient balance
  5. Generate patient statement if balance due

Best Practices

  • ✓ Verify insurance before visit
  • ✓ Submit claims within 24-48 hours
  • ✓ Link diagnoses to procedures
  • ✓ Monitor aging reports weekly
  • ✓ Follow up on claims >30 days
  • ✓ Appeal valid denials promptly
  • ✓ Document payer communications
