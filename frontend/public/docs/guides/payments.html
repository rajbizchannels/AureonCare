Payment Processing

Process and track patient payments for services rendered.

Recording Payments

  1. Go to RCM > Payments
  2. Click + New Payment
  3. Select patient
  4. Enter payment details

Payment Methods

  • Cash: Record cash amount
  • Check: Enter check number
  • Credit/Debit Card: Process via integrated payment gateway
  • ACH/Bank Transfer: Electronic bank payment
  • Patient Portal: Online payment by patient

Payment Types

TypeDescription
CopayFixed amount patient pays at visit
CoinsurancePercentage of bill patient owes
DeductibleAmount before insurance pays
Self-PayFull payment by uninsured patient

Payment Form

Required fields:

  • Patient
  • Amount
  • Payment method
  • Date received
  • Received by (staff member)

Optional:

  • Check number
  • Transaction ID
  • Notes

Posting Payments

  1. Navigate to claim/invoice
  2. Click Post Payment
  3. Enter amount paid
  4. System applies to patient balance
  5. Remaining balance calculated

Refunds

  1. Find payment record
  2. Click Issue Refund
  3. Enter refund amount
  4. Select refund method
  5. Add reason
  6. Process refund

Payment Reports

  • Daily Deposits: Total payments by date
  • By Method: Cash, card, check totals
  • Outstanding Balances: Patients with balances due
  • Payment Trends: Monthly collection trends

Best Practices

  • ✓ Collect copays at check-in
  • ✓ Verify insurance before visit
  • ✓ Offer payment plans for large balances
  • ✓ Send statements within 7 days
  • ✓ Reconcile daily deposits
  • ✓ Document all payments immediately
