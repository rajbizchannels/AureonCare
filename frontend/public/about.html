AureonCare

Practice management and telehealth software for medical clinics

AureonCare is a web-based platform that healthcare providers and clinic staff use to run their day-to-day practice — scheduling appointments, maintaining patient records, e-prescribing medication, submitting insurance claims, and conducting telehealth video visits with patients.

What AureonCare does

Appointment scheduling

Providers manage availability, patients book online, and staff handle waitlists and reminders.

Patient records

Clinical notes, diagnoses, medical documents, and history stored in a HIPAA-aligned record system.

Telehealth visits

Clinicians launch secure video consultations with patients directly from the appointment record.

E-prescribing

Prescriptions are created and routed to the patient's preferred pharmacy.

Billing & claims

Invoices, insurance claims, payment posting, and patient statements.

Patient portal

Patients view upcoming visits, prescriptions, records, and join telehealth sessions.

How AureonCare uses Google APIs

Connecting a Google account to AureonCare is optional. A clinic administrator may connect one so that telehealth appointments booked in AureonCare automatically get a Google Meet video link. The connection is made once, from Admin → Integrations → Google Meet, and can be revoked at any time from the same screen.

Scope requestedWhy AureonCare needs it
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar To create a Google Calendar event containing a Meet conference link when a clinician schedules a telehealth appointment. AureonCare only creates and manages events it generated itself — it does not read, edit, or delete a user's pre-existing calendar events.
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/meetings.space.created To create the Meet space for a telehealth session, retrieve its join link so the provider and patient can enter the visit from AureonCare, and end the space when the session finishes. Access is limited to Meet spaces AureonCare created.

AureonCare does not record meetings, does not access meeting content or participant lists, and does not read calendars or contacts unrelated to appointments it created. Google user data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or to train AI models. OAuth tokens are stored encrypted and are deleted immediately when the administrator disconnects the integration.

Who uses AureonCare

  • Physicians and clinicians — consult with patients, document visits, prescribe medication
  • Clinic administrators — manage staff accounts, integrations, and practice settings
  • Front-desk and billing staff — book appointments, verify insurance, process payments
  • Patients — book visits, view records, and join telehealth sessions through the patient portal
