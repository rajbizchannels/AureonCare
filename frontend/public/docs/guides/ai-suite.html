AI Suite

Found under Settings → Integrations → AI. Administrators configure; everyone uses.

The AI Suite is off by default, every feature is switched on individually, and nothing an AI produces reaches a patient record without a person approving it.

The Governing Rule

Every AI output is a draft until a named reviewer accepts it.

AI never writes to a chart, sends a message, or submits a claim on its own. It produces a suggestion, the suggestion waits in a review queue, and a person with the right role accepts, edits or rejects it. For clinical features, that person must be a clinician.

  1. Go to Settings → Integrations → AI → Providers
  2. Choose the provider and paste your API key
  3. For a cloud provider, confirm you hold a BAA or DPA
  4. Click Test to verify the key
  5. Save

Patient data cannot be sent to a cloud provider until the BAA/DPA confirmation is ticked. This is your practice's legal relationship with that provider, not AureonCare's.

Keys are encrypted at rest and never shown again after saving. To change one, replace it.

If your compliance position does not allow patient data to leave your infrastructure at all, use the offline / self-hosted option.

How Patient Data Is Protected

Before anything is sent, identifying details are replaced with placeholders. The response comes back with placeholders still in it, and AureonCare puts the real values back.

  • The mapping is kept for the duration of one request
  • The originals in that mapping are encrypted
  • An auditor can see exactly which values were substituted
  • Requests are logged under the AI module in the audit trail

Switching a Feature On

  1. Go to Settings → Integrations → AI → Features & cost
  2. Choose the provider and model
  3. Set the reviewer roles who may approve its output
  4. Toggle it on and save

The screen shows estimated cost per use and per month, actual usage this month, and any limit you set.

Tip: Turn on one feature, watch the cost and the review queue for a week, then decide about the next.

The Review Queue

StatusMeaning
PendingWaiting for a reviewer
AcceptedApproved as written
EditedApproved after the reviewer changed it
RejectedDiscarded
ExpiredNobody reviewed it in time
  1. Open the draft
  2. Read it against the source — the transcript, the chart, the document
  3. Accept, Edit, or Reject

Read AI output against its source before accepting. The Ambient Scribe marks uncertainty with [verify], but it cannot flag everything it got wrong. Accepting is a clinical act and is recorded as yours.

Working With AI Drafts

Accepted drafts push straight into the right place:

  • SOAP text onto the clinical note
  • Suggested codes onto the diagnosis
  • Medications to Prescriptions — similar drugs suggested when one is not in the formulary
  • Tests to Lab Orders

AI assist buttons appear on the screens where each feature applies, not in a separate AI area.

Best Practices

  • ✓ Confirm your BAA or DPA before sending patient data anywhere
  • ✓ Start with one or two features and watch the cost
  • ✓ Set reviewer roles deliberately
  • ✓ Clear the review queue daily; expired drafts are wasted spend
  • ✓ Read every clinical draft against its source
  • ✗ Don't accept a draft you have not read
  • ✗ Don't treat [verify] markers as the only things worth checking
  • ✗ Don't give clinical reviewer roles to non-clinical staff to clear the queue faster
